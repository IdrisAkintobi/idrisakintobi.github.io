## 1. Executive Summary & Design Vision

### 1.1 Goal
Create a minimalist, calm, and high-precision personal portfolio for **Idris Akintobi**—a **Senior Backend / Full-Stack Engineer** with **6+ years of experience** architecting high-throughput distributed systems, pan-African telco billing engines, high-compliance fintech platforms, and autonomous AI agent workflows.

### 1.2 Creative Direction & References
- **Aesthetic Tone:** Calm, decluttered, high-precision engineering portfolio. High signal-to-noise ratio, generous whitespace, structural serenity.
- **Inspirations:**
  - **Delta.dev:** Monospace accents, structural minimalism, quiet confidence, clean typography, fast rendering.
  - **Appwrite.io:** Deep graphite dark mode (`#08090C`, `#0A0D14`), subtle ambient glows, crisp borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), developer-first atmosphere.
  - **21st.dev:** High-craft micro-interactions, animated lightning border beams, interactive bento cards, fluid hover states.

- **Motion & Micro-interactions:** **Framer Motion (`motion`)**
